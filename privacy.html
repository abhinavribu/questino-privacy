Questino - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Questino ("the app") helps students, parents and teachers create exam papers, study guides and question-and-answer notes from textbook pages and typed text. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

1. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. The developer does not receive it and has no server that holds it:

  • Your account details: name, email address and role, plus a password and security-question answer that are stored in hashed form.
  • Exams, study guides, notes, reminders, study plans and settings you create.
  • Photos and PDFs you add through the Scanner or exam screens, the text extracted from them, and the results generated from them (Saved Scans).

2. Information sent to Google (Gemini AI)

To read your pages and write questions and answers, the app sends the photos, PDFs and text you choose to process, together with instructions, to Google's Gemini API over an encrypted connection. This is the only time your content leaves your device. The developer does not receive a copy.

Google processes this content under its own terms and privacy policy (ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Please do not upload pages that contain sensitive personal information such as passwords, ID numbers or financial details. If you enter your own Gemini API key in Settings, the app uses your key for these requests.

On-device text recognition (Google ML Kit, used for English and Hindi) runs on your phone and does not send images anywhere.

3. Permissions

  • Camera - pages are photographed with your phone's own camera app; Questino receives only the photo you accept.
  • Photos and files - chosen through Android's system picker; the app cannot browse your other files.
  • Notifications and alarms - to show your reminders and study plans on time.
  • Internet - to reach the Gemini AI service.

4. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, tracking or profiling.
  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • The developer does not receive your account, exams, scans or photos.

5. Children

The app is meant to be used by students together with a parent or teacher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because data stays on the device, a parent or teacher controls what is stored and what is sent for AI processing.

6. Sharing

You can export papers and notes to Word or share them and scan images using your phone's share sheet. What you share, and with whom, is your choice.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete saved exams, scans (including their images), reminders and plans inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all data the app stored on your device. Content already sent to Google for processing is handled under Google's policies. To ask for help with deletion, contact us at the address below.

8. Security

Passwords and security answers are stored as hashes, and communication with the AI service is encrypted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your device protected with a screen lock.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
